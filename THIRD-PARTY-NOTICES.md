# Third-party components


These parts of ID are included under their own licences, not the MIT licence in [LICENSE](LICENSE).

- Standard Urbit libraries and marks (lib/dbug, lib/default-agent,
  lib/docket, lib/server, lib/skeleton, lib/verb, sur/docket, sur/verb, and
  the base marks in mar/), from urbit/urbit. MIT licence.

- lib/sigil.hoon and lib/sigil/symbols.jam, the sigil drawer, from Tlon's
  %groups desk (tlon-apps). MIT licence. Changed as marked "noltbook:" in
  the file.

- qrcode-generator 1.4.4, Copyright (c) 2009 Kazuhiko Arase. MIT licence.
  https://github.com/kazuhikoarase/qrcode-generator
  Inlined at the end of lib/id/index.html.

- jsQR 1.4.0, Copyright 2017 Cosmo Wolfe. Apache License 2.0; the full text
  is in licenses/Apache-2.0.txt. https://github.com/cozmo/jsQR
  Inlined at the end of lib/id/index.html.

- Phone numbering data, taken from the metadata of libphonenumber-js 1.11.14
  (MIT licence, https://gitlab.com/catamphetamine/libphonenumber-js), which
  is derived from Google's libphonenumber (Apache License 2.0,
  https://github.com/google/libphonenumber). Inlined in lib/id/index.html.

- Country names from the Unicode CLDR, via ICU. Unicode License v3,
  https://www.unicode.org/license.txt
